BRHP-MSAM for Barangay Health Workers

Record house visits, health checks, vaccines and supplies on your phone, even with no signal.

Android phones only. Free. Not for iPhone.

How to install

  1. Download

    Tap Download the app above. If your phone says "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    Tap Open when it finishes, or pull down from the top and tap app-release.apk.

  3. Allow and install

    If asked, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back. Then tap Install.

    If Google Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.

  4. Open the app

    Look for BRHP-MSAM with the green logo.

The first time you open it

You need signal for this part only. Sign in with the email and password from your health office, then choose a 4-digit PIN. After that, you only type your PIN to open the app.

Problems?

I forgot my PIN

Your records are safe on the phone. When you have signal, sign in again with your email and password, then choose a new PIN.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap I forgot my password. If you do not see it, ask your health office to reset it.

Were my records sent?

Look at the bar at the top of the app. All records sent means they reached your health office. No signal or Waiting to send means they are safe on your phone and will be sent when you have signal. Could not send: open Home and follow what it says.

How do I update the app?

When a bar says Update is ready, tap Install and follow steps 2 to 3 above. Do not uninstall first: that deletes records not yet sent. If it says Update needed to sync, install it soon, or your records will not be sent.

The download or install does not work

Check that you have signal and free space, then try again. Use Chrome if another browser fails. If it still fails, show this page to your health office.

Still stuck? Ask your barangay or municipal health office.
