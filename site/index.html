What the app does

  • 🏠

    Households and residents

    Add each house you visit and the people who live there.

  • ❤️

    Health checks

    Write down blood pressure, weight, temperature and illness.

  • 💉

    Vaccines

    Keep a record of each vaccine dose given.

  • 📦

    Supplies

    Note the medicine and supplies you give to residents.

  • 📶

    Works with no signal

    Everything saves on your phone. It is sent to your health office by itself once you have signal again.

How to install

You only need to do this once. Use mobile data or Wi-Fi. The download is about 25 MB, so Wi-Fi is best.

  1. Tap the green button

    Tap Download the app above.

    Your phone may warn "File might be harmful". This is normal for apps not from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    When the download finishes, tap Open at the bottom of the screen. If you missed it, pull down from the top of the screen and tap the file app-release.apk.

  3. Allow the install

    The first time, your phone may say it cannot install apps from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press the back button.

  4. Tap Install

    Tap Install and wait a few seconds.

    If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.

  5. Open the app

    Tap Open. Next time, find BRHP-MSAM with the green logo on your home screen or app list.

Using the app

Use the buttons at the bottom of the screen:

HomeSee your recent work and whether your records were sent.
ResidentsAdd a new household, or find a person already recorded.
HealthRecord a health check or a vaccine for a resident.
SupplyRecord medicine or supplies you gave out.
ProfileSee your account, change dark or light screen, or log out.

Were my records sent?

Look at the bar at the top of the app:

All records sentEverything reached your health office.
No signalRecords are safe on your phone. They will be sent when you have signal.
Waiting to sendSending soon. Keep the app open with signal for a moment.
Could not sendOpen Home and follow what it says there.

Updates

When a new version is ready, a bar at the top says Update is ready. Tap Install and follow steps 2 to 5 above. Your records stay on your phone. If it says Update needed to sync, install it soon — your records will not be sent until you do.

The first time you open it

You need signal for this part only.

  1. Sign in

    Type the email and password your health office gave you. No account yet? Ask your health office — you cannot make one yourself.

  2. Choose a 4-digit PIN

    Pick 4 numbers you will remember, then type them again. Do not share your PIN.

  3. Done

    From now on you only type your PIN to open the app, even with no signal.

Problems?

I forgot my PIN

Your records are safe on the phone. When you have signal, sign in again with your email and password, then choose a new PIN.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap I forgot my password. If you do not see it, ask your health office to reset it.

The download or install does not work

Check that you have signal and enough free space, then try again from step 1. Use the Chrome browser if another browser fails. If it still does not work, show this page to your health office.

I have an iPhone

The app only works on Android phones. Ask your health office about a phone you can use.

Should I uninstall the app to update it?

No. Uninstalling deletes records that were not sent yet. Just install the new version on top of the old one.
